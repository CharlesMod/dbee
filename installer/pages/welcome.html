DBee is an on-call engineer that never sleeps.

It sleeps on this machine at no cost, wakes the moment a service fails, reads until it can name the cause, applies one cure it can undo, checks that the cure worked, and goes back to sleep.

It needs a mind to think with. This setup finds one that fits this machine, installs DBee beside it in a folder of its own, and keeps both running. Nothing leaves this machine unless you choose a hosted mind.
